Privacy Policy

Meg is a private application used only by its owner, Chris Dawson. It accesses the owner's own Google account (Calendar, Gmail, Drive, Docs, Sheets and Contacts) solely to perform tasks the owner requests.

No data is collected from any other user. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Data accessed from Google is processed only for the owner's requests and is not stored beyond what the owner asks for.
