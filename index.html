Hermes Assistant MCP

Hermes Assistant MCP is a private personal productivity assistant. It is built for the sole use of its owner and is not offered to the public.

Purpose

The application helps its owner manage their own day-to-day administration. With the owner's permission, it connects to the owner's own account to read and organise personal information, such as schedules, messages and documents, and to carry out tasks the owner explicitly requests, such as adding a calendar entry, finding a document or preparing a draft for the owner to review.

How data is used

Account data is used only to perform the tasks the owner asks for. The application does not collect data from any other person, and does not sell, share or use data for advertising.
